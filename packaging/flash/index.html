小鼠电视FLASH

给设备刷固件Flash a device

把小鼠电视的固件写进 星智 Cube 1.54（小智 AI 4G 款）或 FoloToy AI Passport。在浏览器里完成，不用装软件，固件不会上传到任何地方。

Writes the RooTV firmware to a Xingzhi Cube 1.54 (Xiaozhi AI 4G) or a FoloToy AI Passport, from the browser. Nothing to install; the firmware is not uploaded anywhere.

这个浏览器不能刷机

This browser cannot flash

请在电脑上用 Chrome 或 Edge（桌面版）打开这个页面。手机、平板、Safari、Firefox 都没有网页串口功能。

Open this page on a computer in desktop Chrome or Edge. Phones, tablets, Safari and Firefox have no Web Serial.

01

准备Before you start

  1. 电脑上用 Chrome 或 Edge 打开本页。
  2. 用能传数据的 USB 线把设备接到电脑（有的线只能充电，换一根试）。
  3. 设备保持开机。
  1. Use desktop Chrome or Edge.
  2. Connect the device with a USB data cable (some cables only charge).
  3. Keep the device switched on.
连不上？让设备进入下载模式Not found? Put the device in download mode

星智 Cube 1.54（右侧四个键）：按住从上往下第三个键（BOOT），再按一下最下面的键（复位），然后松开 BOOT。屏幕会黑，这是正常的。再点「连接设备」。

Xingzhi Cube 1.54 (four keys on the right): hold the third key from the top (BOOT), tap the bottom key (reset), release BOOT. The screen goes dark; that is expected. Then press Connect again.

AI Passport：按住电源键约 2 秒关机，再按约 0.5 秒开机，然后再点「连接设备」。它有电池，拔线不会让它重启。

AI Passport: hold the power button about 2 s to switch off, press about 0.5 s to switch on, then press Connect again. It has a battery, so unplugging does not restart it.

弹出的串口列表里一个都没有：换数据线、换 USB 口；Windows 上看看设备管理器里有没有新出现的 COM 口。

The port list is empty: try another cable or USB port; on Windows, check Device Manager for a new COM port.

02

连接设备Connect

点下面的按钮，在弹出的窗口里选中设备（常见名字：USB JTAG/serial debug unit），页面会认出芯片、看看设备里现在装的是什么。这一步只读，不写任何东西。

Press the button and pick the device in the browser's list (usually USB JTAG/serial debug unit). The page identifies the chip and reads what is installed. Nothing is written in this step.

03

确认要做什么Confirm

会先擦除整块芯片

The whole chip will be erased first

这台设备里现在不是小鼠电视的固件（多半是原来的小智 AI 固件）。刷小鼠电视要先把芯片整个擦掉：小智 AI 固件和它的设置都会删掉，之后这台设备就是一台小电视，不再是 AI 对话玩具。

This device is not running the RooTV firmware (most likely the original Xiaozhi AI firmware). The chip is erased completely first: the Xiaozhi AI firmware and its settings are deleted, and the device becomes a small TV instead of an AI chat toy.

想换回去：按小智官方（xiaozhi-esp32 项目）的方法重新刷小智 AI 固件，板型是 xingzhi-cube-1.54tft-ml307，这块板是 8 MB flash。

To go back, re-flash the Xiaozhi AI firmware the way the xiaozhi-esp32 project describes; the board is xingzhi-cube-1.54tft-ml307 with 8 MB of flash.

这次没法保留设置更新

This update cannot keep the settings

只能清空后重装：Wi-Fi、服务器地址要重新设置；设备的密钥会换新，装好、连上网以后，到家庭服务器的管理后台对它点「重新配对」。不想现在做就点「断开」，什么都不会改。

Only a clean reinstall is possible: Wi-Fi and server address must be set again, and the device gets a new secret, so once it is online use "re-pair" on it in the household server's admin. To leave it for now, press Disconnect; nothing is changed.

Passport 从不擦除芯片

A Passport is never erased

写入范围都在 0x356000 的出厂身份信息之前，不会碰到它；上面「设备」一栏的「将要写入」列出了确切的地址。页面写之前会再核对一次范围，越界就不写。

Every write ends before the factory identity at 0x356000, which is never touched; "Will write" above lists the exact addresses. The ranges are checked again before writing.

没读出设备里现在装的是什么

Could not read what is installed

如果这台 Passport 装的是 Muse 双系统（Muse 和电视两个固件并存），不要在这里刷：整块写入会删掉 Muse 和它的配对。那种情况按项目文档里「Passport 与 Muse 并存」一节手动刷。

If this Passport carries the Muse dual setup (Muse beside the TV), do not flash here: a full write deletes Muse and its pairing. Follow the "Passport beside Muse" board document instead.

读不出来就没法只更新固件，只能整个写入：设置区也会写成空白，之后可能要重新配网、在管理后台「重新配对」。可以先断开，让设备重新开机后再连一次试试。

Without reading it, an update that keeps the settings is not possible and the whole image is written, blank settings area included: Wi-Fi setup and re-pairing may follow. You can disconnect, restart the device and connect again first.

04

刷完之后After flashing

先重启设备

Restart the device first

用手机扫码，打开声音配网页

Scan with the phone to open the sound setup page

手机先连上家里的 Wi-Fi，再用相机扫这个码（或者在手机浏览器里输入上面的地址）。「服务器」一栏会自动填好 ，不用改。

Join the home Wi-Fi with the phone first, then scan this code with its camera (or type the address above into the phone's browser). The "Server" field is filled in with ; leave it as it is.

设置和绑定都保留了

Settings and binding were kept

设置区没有被写。重启后它自己连上原来的 Wi-Fi 和家庭服务器，接着放你的频道，不用重新配网，也不用重新配对。

The settings area was not written. After the restart it joins the same Wi-Fi and household server by itself and goes on with your channels; no Wi-Fi setup and no re-pairing.

要是它连上以后一直停在加载画面，或者屏幕上写着 RE-PAIR THIS TV IN ADMIN：家庭服务器不认它的密钥了（以前用会清空设置的方式刷过）。到管理后台的「设备」里对它点「重新配对」，10 分钟内它会自己连上并被记住。

If it then stays on the loading screen, or the screen says RE-PAIR THIS TV IN ADMIN, the household server no longer accepts its secret (an earlier flash cleared the settings). Use "re-pair" on it under Devices in the admin; within 10 minutes it reconnects and is remembered.

重启后屏幕进入「设置」，等你告诉它连哪个 Wi-Fi、找哪台服务器：

After the restart the device opens its setup screens and waits for a Wi-Fi network and a server:

  1. 声音配网（推荐）：手机连上家里的 Wi-Fi，打开家庭服务器的 http://<服务器地址>:8080/tv/wifi（首次设置页里的「设置一台电视」会带你过去），填 Wi-Fi 名和密码，「服务器」一栏填家庭服务器的地址（不用加端口），把手机靠近设备点「发送」。设备听到后会自己连网、重启。
  2. 热点配网（备用）：在设备的设置页里翻到热点那一页，手机用相机扫码连上设备的热点，浏览器打开 192.168.4.1，填 Wi-Fi 和服务器地址。
  3. 绑定：连上服务器后，屏幕显示「绑定这台电视」和一个二维码。用已经登录家庭服务器的手机扫它（或输入屏幕上的码），设备就归你了，开始播你的频道。
  1. Sound setup (recommended): with the phone on your home Wi-Fi, open the household server's http://<server address>:8080/tv/wifi ("Set up a TV" on the first-run page leads there), enter the Wi-Fi name and password, put the household server's address in "Server" (no port needed), hold the phone near the device and press Send. The device joins and restarts.
  2. Hotspot (fallback): on the device's setup screens, find the hotspot page, join it by scanning with the phone's camera, open 192.168.4.1 and enter Wi-Fi and server address.
  3. Bind: once connected, the screen shows "bind this TV" with a QR code. Scan it with a phone signed in to the household server (or type the code) and the device is yours; it starts playing your channels.

手机打不开配网页：看看手机上是不是开着 VPN 或代理（加速器、科学上网一类的软件），关掉再试；手机和这台服务器要连同一个家里的网络。

The phone cannot open the setup page: if a VPN or proxy app is on, turn it off and retry; the phone and this server must be on the same home network.

这台设备以前绑定过：连上服务器后如果一直停在加载画面，或者屏幕上写着 RE-PAIR THIS TV IN ADMIN，到管理后台的「设备」里对它点「重新配对」。

If this device was bound before and, once connected, stays on the loading screen or shows RE-PAIR THIS TV IN ADMIN, use "re-pair" on it under Devices in the admin.

以后更新固件：回到这个页面再刷一次，选「更新固件」，设置区不写，设置和绑定都保留（新版本改了分区表时页面会提前说明，只能清空重装）。

To update later, flash again here and choose Update: the settings area is not written, so settings and binding stay (if a new version changes the partition table, the page says so first and offers only a clean reinstall).

固件是公开版：里面没有任何服务器地址，也不会连向项目作者的服务器；连哪台服务器由你在配网时填写。刷写由 esptool-js 0.6.1（Espressif，Apache-2.0）完成，在你的浏览器里运行。

These are the public builds: they contain no server address and never contact the project author's server; you enter your own during setup. Flashing is done in your browser by esptool-js 0.6.1 (Espressif, Apache-2.0).
