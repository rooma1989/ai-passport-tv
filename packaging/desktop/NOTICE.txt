Xiaozhi TV (小智电视) PC client
==============================

This program is open source under the MIT licence:
https://github.com/rooma1989/ai-passport-tv (LICENSE).

It ships no content: no channel list, no stream address, no playlist, no
cookies, no keys. Everything it plays is added by the person who runs it.
