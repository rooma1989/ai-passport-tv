Xiaozhi TV (小智电视) PC client
==============================

This program is open source under the MIT licence:
https://github.com/rooma1989/ai-passport-tv (LICENSE).

It ships no content: no channel list, no stream address, no playlist, no
cookies, no keys. Everything it plays is added by the person who runs it.

Downloaded only if you turn on YouTube "local mode", not distributed

- yt-dlp, from its official GitHub releases (https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp,
  the standalone yt-dlp_macos / yt-dlp.exe / yt-dlp_linux builds; pinned
  version and SHA-256, updates checked against the release's SHA2-256SUMS).
  yt-dlp is released under the Unlicense; its standalone builds also contain
